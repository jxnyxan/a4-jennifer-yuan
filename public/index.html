📖 How to Play

1. Click Start Game to begin your adventure.

2. Pokémon will appear and move around the field.

3. Click on a Pokémon to throw a Pokéball toward it.

4. Successfully catching Pokémon increases your score.

5. You have 30 Pokéballs. Try to catch as many as possible!

6. Adjust the settings to customize the gameplay.

7. Use Pause to stop the game or Restart to play again.
